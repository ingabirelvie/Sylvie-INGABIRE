About LifeBrook Advisory

A Nutrition & Wellness Consultancy Built for Impact

LifeBrook Advisory Ltd is a nutrition, health, and wellness consultancy based in Kigali, Rwanda. We provide advisory, programme design, and capacity-building services that support preventive health, sustainable lifestyle change, and long-term wellbeing.

🎯

Root Cause Focus

We address the underlying causes of ill health, not just the symptoms, for real and lasting change.

🔗

Integrated Approach

Our work connects nutrition, mental wellbeing, and lifestyle practices into one coherent system.

🌍

People & Community Centred

We serve individuals, families, organisations, and institutions with practical, evidence-based solutions adapted to real-life contexts.

Our Methodology

A Life-Course Approach

We take a life-course approach, recognizing that health is shaped early and influenced continuously. Rather than fragmented care, we provide integrated solutions connecting nutrition, mental wellbeing, and daily habits.

01

Listen

We listen to understand your needs, challenges, and goals deeply before proposing anything.

02

Assess

We assess the context, identify root causes, and review the best available evidence.

03

Design

We co-create customised solutions and action plans that fit real-life situations.

04

Implement & Evaluate

We deliver, support, build capacity, and continuously refine for greater and lasting impact.

Founder Profile

Meet Sabine Umutoniwase

"I believe that health is not just the absence of disease, but a state of balance — physically, mentally, and emotionally. My mission is to help people thrive, not just survive."

Sabine is a Nutritionist, Public Health Specialist, and Founder of LifeBrook Advisory Ltd. With a strong passion for preventive health and sustainable wellbeing, she helps individuals, families, and organizations build healthier lives through practical, evidence-based solutions. Her work integrates nutrition, mental wellbeing, and lifestyle practices to address the root causes of ill health and promote long-term, balanced living.

MSc in Public Health Nutrition – Mount Kenya University
BSc in Human Nutrition – Mount Kenya University
Advanced Training in Nutrition & Wellness
Member, Rwanda Nutritionists & Dieticians Assoc. (RNDA)
Passionate Purpose-Driven Impact Focused Community Minded
